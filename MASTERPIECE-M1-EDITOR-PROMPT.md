## RAISED BAR — "MASTERPIECE" (beyond F1/F4 "premium")
Premium was the floor. Masterpiece means: a designer would screenshot this and
ask what studio made it. Judge each surface on: composition + visual hierarchy;
material believability under motion (refraction/specular respond to camera and
hover); micro-interaction delight (easing, weight, settle — nothing linear);
typographic elegance; cohesion (toolbar, keyframe, dock, labels read as ONE
crafted instrument); and restraint (no gimmick per DESIGN-REFERENCES law —
dogfood the primitive toolkit: TSL/WebGPU materials, refraction-glass,
GSAP/cinematic motion — not CSS approximations).
