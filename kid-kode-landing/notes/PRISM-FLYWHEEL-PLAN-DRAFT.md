## 1. Prism's unfair advantage (the enhancement of the concept)
Everyone can log prompts. Almost no one has what Prism has natively: a
VERIFY LOOP. Every node build passes behavioral/visual/deploy checks, dual
judges, and self-heal cycles before "shippable." That means every episode is
OUTCOME-LABELED at birth — which is precisely the scarce ingredient for
Reinforcement Fine-Tuning (RFT). Fresh-verified: RFT is now a first-class
product (Fireworks bills it per GPU-hr for outcome-reward training; Amazon
Nova Forge does RL against customer environments; a 2026 paper shows RFT in a
narrow technical domain lifted correctness +33pts over its own base model).
Prism's build-verify harness IS an RL environment. We don't have to build
one — we have to instrument the one we already have.
