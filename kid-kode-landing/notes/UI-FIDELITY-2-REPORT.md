## Plain-language summary

The editor's surfaces are no longer pictures of materials — they are materials.
Every toolbar, panel, pill and key is now drawn by the same photoreal renderer
that draws the scene: brushed metal with real micro-striations (generated by
fal.ai and poured into the shader), smoked glass that genuinely bends and blurs
whatever the scene shows behind it, a brass keyline that is lit geometry, and a
warm light that rides your cursor across all of it. Typography moved from
default-looking fonts to a deliberate three-face system. Mode and page changes
sweep a real pane of glass across the screen instead of swapping panels.

And the first thing a new user sees is no longer a gray slab with a black watch
silhouette — it's ORRERY No.7: a five-chapter luxury timepiece showcase with a
photoreal generated watch turning under real lights, molten brass poured into
the headline letterforms, a seamless molten-metal video moment, planets wearing
generated stone textures, and animation bound across every driver the engine
has.

## Honest flags / carried residuals

1. **Perf:** glass refraction over budget in dev harness (above) — W4 residual:
   GPU timestamps + half-res copy on a production build.
2. **Primitive bugs found by the advocate loop (catalog backlog):**
   `scroll-stagger-rise`, `pointer-shine`, `embers` spawn untextured twin quads
   on mounted artifacts; `scroll-depth-dolly` drives a degenerate transform
   (subject vanishes); `decode-text` can stick mid-scramble. All four excluded
   from the showcase; they need engine fixes in the Animatable layer.
3. **Mobile:** t1 fallback clean, but the showcase is authored desktop-first —
   narrow viewports clip the wide compositions (responsive breakpoints exist in
   schema, un-authored). Same behavior class as the previous demo.
4. **Standalone catalog route frames** not slab-hosted (no GraphScene canvas there).
5. **nodeTexture.ts galaxy-sphere text** still canvas2D (redesign-class backlog);
   HubLabels canvas2D was killed (real MSDF now).
6. **Advocate taste flags (non-blocking, carried):** Add-Node ink contrast
   2.67:1 at t2; hub1 drive-viewport text tightening; transient watch-over-
   subline during pointer/event beats.
7. **Catalog primitive controls under load:** the parallel harness's controls
   check is sensitive to machine load (650 ms settle); ~90 false flags when
   the operator works alongside the run. Consider an adaptive settle or
   a dedicated-machine convention for fresh runs.
